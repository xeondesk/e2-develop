# Nexo Vision

Nexo evolves from a legacy PHP CMS into an open Content Operating System.

## Mission
Make content programmable, portable, extensible, observable, secure, and AI-ready.

## Long-term outcomes
CMS → Headless CMS → Content Platform → Content OS → AI-native content ecosystem.

## Product surfaces
Web, Admin, Studio, API, CLI, SDKs, plugins, themes, cloud, AI.
